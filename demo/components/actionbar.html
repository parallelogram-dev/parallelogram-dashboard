Action Bar

A segmented bar standing for one record, with a reorder handle, title, switches, dropdowns and actions.

Playground

Examples

An action bar stands for one record. Segments are joined by keylines: .actionbar__handle to reorder, .actionbar__select for bulk selection, .actionbar__title (label, meta and badge), then switches, dropdowns and actions. Mark segments that may fold into the "⋯" menu with data-priority="low" (folds first) or "medium": they fold before the bar's .actionbar__label would be cut off, and every bar in a list, nested lists included, folds together so the columns stay aligned. Folded segments become menu items (a switch becomes a tickable item kept in sync); data-label sets their menu text. This is the same data-overflow-actions behaviour tables use for row actions. Put bars in <ul class="actionbar__list" data-actionbar-sortable> to reorder them by dragging a handle, or focus a handle and press Space, then the up and down arrows. With data-actionbar-sortable="tree" the nested lists inside become one tree: drop onto the middle of a bar to nest an item under it, and use the right and left arrows to nest or un-nest. An .actionbar__toggle with aria-controls expands and collapses a child list. The root list fires actionbar:reorder with { item, from, to, fromParent, toParent }.
