Bulk Actions

Bar of actions for the records selected in a table or record grid, shown while anything is selected.

Usage

.bulkactions acts on the records selected in a table or record grid. It holds a bulkactions__count, a bulkactions__actions group of small buttons and dropdowns, and optionally a bulkactions__clear button that empties the selection.

Put the bar in the page with the hidden attribute. The page's own script updates the count, removes hidden once a row is selected and adds it back when none are; the bar slides in each time it appears (not with reduced motion). By default it floats over the bottom of the viewport, full width on a phone, so its dropdowns open upwards with dropdown--top. bulkactions--inline sits in the flow instead, for example above the table in place of its toolbar.
