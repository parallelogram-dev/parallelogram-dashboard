Flash messages, a wordmark and information rows

.dashboard__alerts stacks flash messages above the page content. .subnav__logo puts a wordmark in the section menu’s header in place of its title, at --subnav-logo-height (1.5rem). .subnav__item--static is a row that reports rather than links, .subnav__item--muted greys it out, and .subnav__item-text--stacked holds a .subnav__item-primary line over a .subnav__item-secondary one. The playground’s Overview with notices preset shows them in the shell.
