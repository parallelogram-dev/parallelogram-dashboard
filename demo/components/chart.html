Data display

Chart

Line, area, bar and stacked bar charts drawn from a data table.

Playground

Examples

Wrap a data <table> in <figure class="chart" data-chart="line">. The first column holds the x labels and each other column is a series; put data-value on cells whose text is formatted, and set data-chart-format to number, currency or percent. Without JavaScript the table shows. With it, main.js draws the chart, a legend for two or more series, a crosshair or per-bar tooltip, arrow-key reading and a Table toggle. Series colours come from --chart-1 to --chart-8 in fixed order, with separate light and dark steps.
