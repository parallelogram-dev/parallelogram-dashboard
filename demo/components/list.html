Data display

List

Rows with an optional status, a title and description, and trailing actions. Use .list--segment to divide rows with rules, .list--boxed to give each row its own box, or .list--disc and .list--spaced for plain lists. The status and actions line up with the first line of the title, like a bullet; add .list--center to centre them on the whole row instead. An empty .list__status shows a dot; put an icon inside it to show that instead.
