With unsaved-changes tracking

Edit a field: its tab gets a dot and the badge appears. Put it back and both clear. Unsaved changes

Add data-unsaved-guard to a form or editing area. While any field differs from its saved value the area has data-dirty, so does the .tabs__trigger of any panel holding a change (drawn as a dot), anything marked data-unsaved-indicator shows, and leaving the page asks first. Fields inside data-unsaved-ignore don't count. After saving, dispatch unsaved:reset on the area; it fires unsaved:change with { dirty } when its state flips.
