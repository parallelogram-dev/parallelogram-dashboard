Record Grid

Picture view of a record list: image-led tiles with a title, status badges, an action menu and a selection checkbox.

Usage

.recordgrid is the picture view of a record list, a <ul> of recordgrid__item tiles that fill the row at a minimum width of 14rem (recordgrid--sm: 10rem). Each tile is led by a recordgrid__thumb (4:3, or square with recordgrid--square) holding an image that fills the frame, or with recordgrid__thumb--contain sits whole inside it; a record with no image puts an .icon there instead. The recordgrid__body holds a recordgrid__title, one or more recordgrid__meta lines and a recordgrid__footer with the recordgrid__status badges and the record's action dropdown.

A recordgrid__select checkbox over the thumbnail lets the grid share the table's bulk actions. It shows while the tile is hovered or focused, and on every tile once any is checked. A checked tile is outlined; aria-disabled="true" dims an unpublished or locked record without disabling its controls.
