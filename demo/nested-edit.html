How this mock-up works

Projects are records of their own, picked through a lookup. Everything nested opens in one side sheet that steps deeper and back, never a second overlay on top. Pickers end in Done, which hands the choice back to the form beneath. Records end in Save, which saves that record straight away. A project's own lookups go one level deeper; past that, links open the record on its own page.

    Rules this mock-up follows

    1. One layer above the page. Going deeper replaces the sheet's contents, with Back and a breadcrumb.
    2. Pickers end in Done and records end in Save.
    3. Shared records say so before you change them.
    4. Escape goes up one level and asks before losing changes.
    5. No more than two levels inside the sheet; deeper records open on their own page.
